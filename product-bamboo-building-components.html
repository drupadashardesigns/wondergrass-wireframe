Bamboo Panels

Wonder Grass offers a range of panels and screens made from bamboo to serve a variety of functional and aesthetic needs.

Request a Quote Explore Components
Home›Products›Bamboo Panels

Screens and panels made with bamboo have traditionally been used in our houses and adorned our built spaces. At Wonder Grass we decided to take this tradition a step further by creating a range of panels and screens which will complement a contemporary design idiom and are ecologically sustainable.

Product 1

Green Wall Panel — GWP

Prefabricated flat panel assemblies for walls, partitions, facades, and decorative screening applications.

Green Wall Panel — GWP

A standardised modular panel made from bamboo poles — emerging as a sustainable, friendly and aesthetically elegant choice for architectural applications.

The panels can be mounted on the existing compound or a parapet-wall, with the help of specially designed metal joinery.

  • Available in 3 standard sizes: 2'×6' | 4'×6' | 6'×6'
  • Available in Natural finish (PU coated) or CNSL coated
  • Borate-treated for pest and fungal resistance
  • Lightweight and easy to handle on-site
  • Can be combined with other structural elements
