Our Sectors

Who We Build For

We serve clients across five sectors — each with specific bamboo building needs that our standardised product range is designed to meet.

Agriculture

Agricultural Infrastructure

Farm sheds and poly-shed net systems for farmers, horticulturalists, and agri-businesses. Our BIY Kits and shade net structures are built for field conditions — durable, cost-effective, and fast to assemble.

Explore Products →

Hospitality

Eco-Resorts & Hospitality

Eco-Lite transit living units and B-POD cottages for resort developers, farmstays, glamping sites, and wellness retreats seeking distinctive, sustainable bamboo accommodation.

Explore Products →

Institutional

Institutional & Community Spaces

Large-span bamboo structures for NGOs, community organisations, educational institutions, and public spaces — designed and built using engineered bamboo building components.

Explore Products →

Residential

Residential & Farmhouses

Two cottage series for private clients: B-POD cottages, raised above ground on a bamboo sub-structure; and the Barnhaus series, built on-plinth — both suited for agricultural land and farm retreat settings.

Explore Products →

Events

Events & Temporary Structures

Bamboo temporary structures — stages, pavilions, entrance gateways, and shade canopies — for festivals, exhibitions, and corporate events. Flat-packable, quick to assemble, and fully reusable.
